Mandatory Government & College Regulations

  • Deadline: All government scholarship application forms must be submitted online/at the office before 31st July.
  • Course Switch Rule: Students who leave a course incomplete midway cannot claim a scholarship for a new course.
  • Professional Course Rule: Students coming from professional courses are not eligible for concessions in non-professional courses.
  • Dual Claim Restriction: A student can avail only one government scholarship or concession at a time.
