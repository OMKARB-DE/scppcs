Before You Apply

  • Verify your qualifying examination eligibility before registration.
  • Keep original certificates and attested photocopies ready.
  • Ensure scanned passport photographs and signatures adhere to specifications.
  • Regularly check admission notices and official portal updates.
  • Double-check spelling of candidate name, date of birth, and category details.
  • Keep application login credentials and transaction receipts secure.
  • Complete fee payments well before stated deadlines to avoid technical failures.
